01 · The job

We build every
digital moment
between a fan
and the gates.

Our job is to turn interest into tickets, tickets into great days and great days into lifelong fans, then prove it with data.

04 · The digital marketing lifecycle

One fan.
Eight moments.

This is the loop we run for every fan of every brand. Scroll through each stage to see which teammates light up and which channels do the work.

01 / 08
Discover
JohnnyRoshniGreg
01

Discover

Can fans find us, and trust what they find?

Fans start on Google, ChatGPT, YouTube and TikTok. We make sure our official sites are the source those platforms trust, with accurate pages, structured data and content shaped around real fan questions.

WebsitesSearchAI answersApp storesVideo
On the fieldOur goal is simple. Be the source fans and AI trust.
02

Capture

How does a visitor become a known fan?

Every sign-up form, event activation and deposit flow sends fan data straight into Iterable. SMS consent gets captured from day one because it can never be retrofitted later.

Sign-up formsDigiohSMS consentIterable
On the fieldatlcty.com launched with forms wired directly into Iterable so campaigns can be targeted and measured from the first click.
03

Welcome

What happens in the first 72 hours?

New fans get welcome journeys tailored to where they came from, whether that's a grassroots event, a deposit or a Stay Connected form. Timely follow-up turns curiosity into commitment.

EmailSMSJourneys
On the fieldNWSL welcome journeys follow up by email at 48 hours and SMS at 72 hours to keep conversion momentum.
04

Engage

Why would a fan keep opening?

Weekly newsletters, account updates, tune-in reminders and fresh app content keep fans connected between big moments. Every send goes through the same review standard.

Weekly emailApp contentPushWeb
On the fieldFinal Review and Approval checks the from name, subject line, preheader, every link, date, image and contact list before anything is scheduled.
05

Convert

When is a fan ready to buy?

High-propensity, handraiser, abandoned browse and single-game buyer journeys meet fans when intent is highest. Paid builds demand and owned channels convert and qualify.

Triggered journeysSMSRetargetingPersonalization
On the fieldFalcons SMS drove $301K in ticket revenue in 2025. That's why SMS is treated as a primary conversion lever.
06

Event Day

Does the fan know exactly what to do?

Parking, gates, policies, food, accessibility and wayfinding. The most important information we publish all year lands on event day, and it has to be right.

KBYG emailAppDigital mapChatbotEvent pages
On the fieldOur new digital stadium map tested live at Sunday Night Football before its planned fan launch for the 49ers game on Oct 25.
07

Retain

Will they come back and renew?

Season ticket members, Founding Members and lapsed fans each get their own path. Service recovery journeys protect renewals, and re-engagement tracks make sure the funnel never dead-ends.

Renewal journeysService recoveryMember comms
On the fieldFounding Members for Atlanta City FC are treated as a distinct audience with exclusive creative and priority communication.
08

Measure

What did we learn, and what changes next?

Delivery, opens, clicks, conversions and revenue attribution feed the next plan. We test, learn and share results so every brand gets smarter.

KPIsDashboardsA/B testsAI insights
On the fieldWe report regularly on platform performance, journey insights and new tactics as we move to a more personalized, journey-centric model.

07 · Arthur M. Blank core values

How we
show up.

Six core values run every business in the Blank Family of Businesses. Tap a card to see how our team lives each one, and where we're still pushing ourselves to get better.

01

Put People First

People are the heartbeat of a successful enterprise. Build trust, listen and invest in growth.

Tap to flip ↻
How we live it

Teammates get a real seat at the table in vendor evaluations, demos, training and cross-functional meetings. That exposure builds networks, confidence and an understanding of how our work connects to the business.

Where we're sharpeningMaking sure great work earns more space, development and recognition, not just more work.
02

Include Everyone

Our differences make us more valuable. Harness them for a competitive edge.

Tap to flip ↻
How we live it

We design for fans who reach us in different ways, across web, email, SMS, push, apps, chatbots, maps and video. We pull in the people closest to the work, from operations to service to data, before we decide.

Where we're sharpeningBringing subject matter experts in earlier so inclusion means real participation, not just review.
03

Lead by Example

Show an entrepreneurial spirit. Make decisions, take responsibility and speak up.

Tap to flip ↻
How we live it

Many of our biggest initiatives started because someone raised a hand and suggested a better path. From the Iterable migration to app platforms to AI, this team steps into unfamiliar territory and owns outcomes, not just tasks.

Where we're sharpeningSlowing down just enough to debate alternatives before we jump into execution.
04

Listen and Respond

Understand what customers need and treat every need as a golden opportunity.

Tap to flip ↻
How we live it

We work to understand objectives, constraints, timing and event-day realities before choosing the digital response. A request is a chance to find the underlying problem, not just deliver the first thing asked for.

Where we're sharpeningHearing from more voices, not just the most confident ones in the room.
05

Innovate Continuously

Non-stop reinvention. Don't let bureaucracy kill innovation.

Tap to flip ↻
How we live it

We challenge legacy processes, explore emerging technology and move the organization from general interest in AI to practical uses in copy, journeys, personalization and operations.

Where we're sharpeningDefining the problem, success metrics and long-term ownership before an experiment becomes a product.
06

Give Back to Others

The well-being of business can't be separated from the well-being of society.

Tap to flip ↻
How we live it

Our team mentors, joins Associate Resource Groups and coaches colleagues on digital, analytics and AI. Our work with Atlanta City FC helps grow the visibility of women's sports and build a future community asset for Atlanta.

Where we're sharpeningCreating more ways to give back together as a team, and training future leaders across AMBSE.
